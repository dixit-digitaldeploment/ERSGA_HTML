Electric Lineman Apprenticeship Program

Help ensure our community has reliable and efficient electric services 24/7.

New Braunfels Utilities offers an Electric Lineman Apprentice Program and training. The NBU Electric Lineman Apprentice Program is certified by the Department of Labor and provides a four-year progressive training platform that includes both on-the-job training and classroom/online learning courses.

To Qualify, You Must have:

  • A High School Diploma or GED
  • A current Class "A" CDL
  • Completed a Pole Climbing Course from a certified agency
  • One to three years of experience

Help Ensure our community has reliable and efficient electric services 24/7.

Pre-apprenticeship Training Program

Groundsmen are hired at an entry level and are provided the opportunity to obtain the qualifications stated above. All candidates, internal or external, considered for admission must demonstrate a range of skills and abilities to be accepted in the Apprenticeship Program.

Need More Info?

For more information about Apprenticeship requirements and the application process, contact us via email or at 830.629.8495.
